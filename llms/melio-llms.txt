# Melio AI-Friendly Content Guide

>Melio is America's fastest-growing bill pay and invoicing solution, powering payments for millions of businesses and their vendors—both directly and through leading banks, accounting firms, and SaaS platforms. Melio turns complex business payments into simple, automated workflows and empowers business owners and accountants all around the US with better cash flow management, control and growth opportunities.

## Services

—[Accounts Payable](https://melio.com/accounts-payable/) Complete AP automation with bill capture, approval workflows, and flexible payment methods including ACH, card, wire transfers, and checks
—[Accounts Receivable](https://melio.com/accounts-receivable/) Invoice creation, payment requests, and customer payment processing with direct bank deposit
—[Fast Payments](https://melio.com/fast-payments/) Multiple payment methods including instant transfers, same-day payments, international payments, and pay-over-time options
—[Pay Over Time](https://melio.com/pay-over-time/) Flexible payment options allowing businesses to pay bills in up to 12 monthly installments. Vendors get paid on time while businesses manage cash flow. Subject to eligibility and approval.
